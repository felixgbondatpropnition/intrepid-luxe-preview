We have just returned from a fabulous three night stay in Ercolano in Italy, visiting Pompeii, Herculaneum and Naples.

Having always previously booked family trips myself, often later than I’d ideally like to have planned, it was a joy to pass over the responsibility to Emilie. She was so professional, swiftly coming up with a selection of perfectly judged hotel options, flights and itineraries. Once we had selected our hotel and flights she kept us fully up to date and advised on potential trips and guided visits. Our three night trip felt a lot longer in all the very best ways!

The hotel was a hidden gem with beautiful gardens, a huge sea-view balcony and excellent restaurant, our guided visits with archaeologists were hugely informative and rewarding and we fitted in a wine tasting and a fascinating day in Naples visiting museums and churches too. We highly recommend Intrepid Luxe and are already thinking about where we might travel to next. First class service from Intrepid Luxe and five stars from us. Thank you Emilie.
