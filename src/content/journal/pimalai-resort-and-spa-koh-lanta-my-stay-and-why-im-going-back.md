Some hotels make you happy as soon as you arrive. For me, Pimalai Resort & Spa on Koh Lanta is one of them. I tend to notice the little things when I stay somewhere, and here the details kept giving me reasons to smile, from the warm welcome to the lovely scent when we opened the door to our room.

Our stay came about by chance. When we enquired months earlier, the resort was fully booked. Then, in March 2026, the conflict in Iran disrupted our journey through the Middle East and left us stranded in Thailand. A room came up at Pimalai, and we took it. Having stayed, I understand why people book so far ahead.

The resort sits on a green hillside above the beach, with a beautiful view out over the sea. It has an unassuming charm that owes as much to the people as it does to the setting. Everyone we met was warm and quietly efficient, and the service felt seamless. After all the uncertainty around our travel plans, it felt especially good to be so well looked after.

Even our entry-level room felt luxurious, with everything we needed right where we wanted it. Breakfast was included and was one of the best we’ve had anywhere. There was such a good choice of dishes cooked to order that deciding what to have became part of the pleasure.

The main pool looks out over the sea, but my favourite mornings were the ones we spent down at the beach, taking out the complimentary paddleboards. The spa is tucked into the greenery, and there are padel courts, a well-equipped gym and a Muay Thai ring where you can learn from professionals.

Pimalai has earned two MICHELIN Keys and appeared several times in the *Condé Nast Traveler* Readers’ Choice Awards. Its spa has won a World Luxury Spa Award, and the resort also received recognition in the 2024 Travel & Hospitality Awards’ Food & Restaurant programme. Having spent time there, I can see why the praise keeps coming.

Pimalai is one of the finest hotels I’ve stayed in, and I still think about those mornings on the water. We’ve already booked our next stay, well in advance this time. I’m already imagining that first glimpse of the resort from the speedboat…
