On recent flights, I’ve experienced the newer airport security scanners, and they’ve substantially shortened my time getting through security. Less unpacking and repacking makes the whole process feel smoother, and everything seems to run more efficiently. It’s made a real difference to the start of my trips.

The restrictions began in August 2006 after authorities foiled a terrorist plot to use liquid explosives on passenger aircraft. This prompted tighter security measures and, eventually, the familiar 100ml container limit.

For 20 years, flying has meant squeezing toiletries into small containers, finding a resealable plastic bag and pulling everything out for inspection. Thankfully, that’s changing.

Major UK airports including Heathrow, Gatwick, Birmingham, Bristol and Edinburgh now allow liquid containers of up to two litres to remain inside your hand luggage during screening.

The rules still vary, though. Manchester and Luton allow liquids to stay inside your bag but retain the 100ml container limit. Check the requirements for every airport on your journey, including your return flight.

It’s great to see changes when they make a positive difference, and I think everyone will certainly welcome this huge improvement.
