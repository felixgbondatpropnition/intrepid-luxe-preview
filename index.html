Welcome to Intrepid Luxe

A little luxury along the way

I’m Emilie, founder of Intrepid Luxe, an independent travel business, dedicated to creating personalised holidays and unforgettable experiences.

The name brings together everything I love about travel: the excitement of discovering somewhere new, with a little luxury along the way. I believe every holiday should feel personal to you, and planning it should be part of the enjoyment.

Whether you’re dreaming of a luxury escape, a family holiday, a romantic getaway, a cruise or a once-in-a-lifetime adventure, I’m here to bring your ideas to life.

How to book with Intrepid Luxe

Your next holiday, made simple

Whether you know exactly where you want to go or need a little inspiration, I’ll guide you through four simple steps.

  1. 1

    Let’s chat

    Tell me about your destination ideas, preferred dates, budget and the kind of holiday you have in mind. From relaxing beach breaks and city stays to tailor-made adventures and special celebrations, we’ll explore what suits your style.

  2. 2

    Leave the research to me

    I’ll compare accommodation, prices and experiences through my network of almost 100 trusted travel suppliers to create a personalised quote. Your trip can include flights, accommodation, transfers, car hire and excursions, alongside theme park visits, sporting events and thoughtful extras.

  3. 3

    Make it yours

    We’ll review the options together and fine-tune the details until the holiday feels right for you. I’ll offer honest advice throughout, with no pressure to book.

  4. 4

    Relax and look forward

    Once you’re happy, I’ll take care of the booking arrangements and travel details. I’ll remain your personal point of contact before, during and after your holiday, with support if you need it.

What does it cost?

My travel planning and booking service comes at no extra cost to you. I receive commission from the travel suppliers when you book, so you benefit from my research, knowledge and personal service without an additional planning fee.

Depending on your booking, applicable ABTA and ATOL protection may also be available.

Why book with Intrepid Luxe?

Expert guidance. A personal touch.

  • More time for you

    I take care of the research, comparisons and planning, leaving you free to enjoy the anticipation of your next adventure.

  • A holiday designed around you

    At the heart of Intrepid Luxe is a personal, one-to-one service. I take the time to understand what matters to you, offering honest recommendations that reflect your preferences, priorities and budget.

    My trusted supplier network opens up a wide choice of holidays and special offers at competitive prices, including trade-only deals. Depending on your booking, you may also benefit from exclusive extras such as room upgrades or added benefits, where available.
